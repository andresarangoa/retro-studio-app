Retro Studio: Beat Maker

Privacy policy

Effective November 4, 2026.

This policy covers the iPhone and iPad app Retro Studio (com.retrostudio.musicduo). The developer is Andres Arango Rodriguez.

Data we collect

None. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app does not send anything to our servers or to third parties.

What stays on your device

Your projects, patterns, theme and settings are saved only on your device. Audio files you import are chosen by you in the system file picker. Exports and shares only happen when you tap Share.

Purchase

The app is a one-time paid download processed by Apple. We never receive your card or billing details.
