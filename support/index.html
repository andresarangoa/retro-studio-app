Retro Studio: Beat Maker

Support

Retro Studio is a portable music studio: step sequencer, synthesizer, drum pads, samples, mixer and effects, with five themes (Analog Tape, Cyberpunk, Nature Lo-Fi, Studio Pro and Vinyl).

Getting started

  • Pick a theme on first launch. You can change it later in Settings.
  • Open the Sequencer, tap steps to build a pattern and press Play.
  • Use the Synthesizer and Drums tabs to play live, and the Mixer and Effects tabs to shape the sound.
  • Save your work in Projects and share the export from the Studio screen.
